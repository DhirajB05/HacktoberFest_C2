About & Privacy

How TrailEar Works

  • All bird call detection happens locally on your device
  • No audio is recorded or transmitted
  • GPS location is stored optionally and only on your device
  • Session data lives in a local SQLite database

Models Used

  • BirdNET-Analyzer for bird call detection (local inference)
  • Gemma/Qwen/Llama via Ollama or llama.cpp for summaries (optional)
